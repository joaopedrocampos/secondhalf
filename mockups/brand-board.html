Never — treatments tried and rejected

  • A dot or ring on the line (reads as a pokéball button)
  • A split-colour centre spot (reads as a dart target)
  • A gap between the halves (splits the mark into two objects)
  • Two colours in one mark (the halfway split turns messy)
  • Swapping the halves (solid = played, open = yours; never reverse)
  • Trimming the line to the circle (the overshoot is the pitch)
