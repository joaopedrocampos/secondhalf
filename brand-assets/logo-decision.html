The pitch-line take

The halfway line runs through the circle — and keeps going

Why this kills the pokéball for good: a pokéball's line never leaves the ball. A pitch's halfway line always crosses through the centre circle and continues. The overshoot turns the mark from "an object" into "field markings" — first half played, second half open, unmistakably a pitch.
